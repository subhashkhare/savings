Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your financial information is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use it to track expenses and savings, how we use that information, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Financial entries: expenses, amounts, categories, dates, notes, and savings goals that you add.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited, used to keep the service working well.

Spendly does not ask for, and does not need, your bank login details, card numbers, or account passwords.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store your expenses and savings goals and show your reports and summaries.
  • To keep the service secure and to detect and prevent misuse.
  • To contact you about important account or service updates.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the service is used so we can improve it.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not share your expense entries with advertisers.
  • We do not use your financial entries to show you targeted ads.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only when necessary: with trusted service providers who help us operate Spendly (such as hosting), under strict confidentiality; when required by law or a valid legal request; or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the service may not work without them.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including hashed passwords and restricted access to stored data. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expense and savings data will be removed, except where we are required to keep certain records by law.

8. Your rights and choices

  • View and update your account details at any time.
  • Edit or delete individual expense entries and savings goals.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.

9. Children's privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. You can also read our Terms & Conditions.
